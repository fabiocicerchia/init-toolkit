## [0.1.0]

### Added

- `wait-for`: single front door for TCP/HTTP readiness checks.
- `wait-for-it` and `dockerize`, bundled unmodified from upstream.
- `healthcheck-http` / `healthcheck-tcp` one-line `HEALTHCHECK` probes.
